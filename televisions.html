Television Energy Efficiency Analysis

Under the Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (GEMS) Act, televisions sold in Australia must carry an Energy Rating Label displaying star ratings from 1 to 10 and annual energy consumption estimates.

LCD (LED) Dominance
Accounts for over 84% (3,485 models) of all registered televisions in Australia.
Top Screen Sizes
55" and 65" panels represent the highest frequency in the market.
Power by Tech
Median power draw ranges from ~71W (LCD) to ~113W (OLED).
